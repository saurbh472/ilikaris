Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with iLikaris.

How does the project onboarding process work?
We start with a complimentary 15–20 minute discovery call to understand your goals, requirements, and technical constraints. Afterward, I provide a detailed proposal covering technical architecture, timeline milestones, and deliverables. Once approved, development begins immediately in agile sprints.
How do you protect private company data when building AI models?
Data privacy is our top priority. We use zero-retention enterprise LLM endpoints, local open-source models (such as Llama / Mistral) deployed within your private VPC, or self-hosted vector databases. Your sensitive company data is never used to train public models, and all interactions are encrypted.
Who owns the code and intellectual property?
You do. 100% of the source code, designs, documentation, and intellectual property belong completely to you and your company upon milestone completion.
What is the typical timeline for an MVP or custom web application?
Most MVPs and custom web systems take between 2 to 6 weeks from kickoff to production launch, depending on the complexity of features, integrations, and AI pipelines. We establish transparent weekly sprint goals so you always know where things stand.
Do you provide post-launch maintenance and support?
Yes! Every fixed-scope project includes a 30-day warranty period for bug fixes and adjustments. For ongoing feature development, infrastructure monitoring, and continuous improvements, we offer flexible monthly engineering retainer packages.
