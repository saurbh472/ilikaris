OUR MISSION & ORIGIN

Dismantling the Broken Agency Model

Most companies hiring software agencies experience the same disappointing loop: you speak with charismatic sales executives during onboarding, only to have your mission-critical product handed off to junior developers, bloated management tiers, and endless status meetings.

iLikaris was founded on a simple antithesis: Pair ambitious product teams directly with a battle-tested Senior Engineer. No middlemen. No outsourced juniors. Just high-velocity execution, rigorous architecture, and complete technical ownership from day one.

We operate remotely and seamlessly across European, North American, and APAC time zones, providing daily transparency, production-grade code reviews, and enterprise data security.
